# UK legal and platform checklist (plain English, not legal advice)

Researched 21 Oct 2026. Rules change. Check the linked official pages before you act, and get
professional advice for tax or contracts.

## 1. Ads, affiliate links and freebies (ASA / CAP and CMA)
- If you get **anything** for a mention (money, commission, free products, a free subscription),
  the post is an advert and must be **obviously identifiable**.
- Put **"Ad"** or **#ad** at the **start** of the caption, not buried in the hashtags. On Instagram,
  also use the **Paid partnership** label for brand deals.
- Affiliate links count. "#affiliate" or "#aff" on its own isn't enough. Use "Ad".
- Say it in the video too ("this is an ad" or "I earn a commission from this link") for video-only viewers.
- `factory.py check` enforces the caption rule when a script sets `disclosure.affiliate` or `.sponsored`.
- Source: ASA/CAP *Influencers' guide to making clear that ads are ads* -
  https://www.asa.org.uk/resource/influencers-guide.html

## 2. Money and income claims
- No guaranteed income, "get rich", "risk-free" or "anyone can" claims. The script checker blocks these.
- Only quote income figures with evidence, and never imply they're typical.
- **Don't** create fake earnings screenshots, fake testimonials or fake reviews. Fake reviews are
  specifically banned under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024, and misleading
  claims break consumer protection law and the CAP Code.
- Stay away from investment, trading and crypto advice. That's FCA-regulated territory.
  "Financial promotions" need FCA authorisation.

## 3. AI-generated likeness
- Instagram/Meta: turn on the **AI label** for realistic AI-generated video.
- YouTube: answer **Yes** to "altered or synthetic content" for realistic synthetic people or voices.
- Only use your own face and voice, or someone's with written permission.

## 4. Copyright
- Research other creators, but don't re-upload, download or closely copy their videos, scripts or
  catchphrases. The research log stores *paraphrased* hooks only.
- Music: use each app's built-in licensed library. Don't add commercial songs in editing.
- Stock clips and images: use properly licensed sources and keep a note of the licence.
- Screenshots of tools are usually fine for commentary or demos. Avoid using brand logos in a way
  that suggests the brand endorses you.

## 5. Privacy and data (UK GDPR, PECR)
- If you build an email list (for a lead magnet), you need consent, a privacy notice, and an
  easy unsubscribe. See the ICO's direct marketing guidance:
  https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/
- Blur names and faces in any screenshot of messages, reviews or dashboards.
- If you sell AI services that handle client customer data, you may be a data processor.
  Use a simple written agreement.

## 6. Tax (HMRC)
- Income from content, affiliates, digital products and services is taxable. The £1,000
  **trading allowance** may cover very small amounts. Above it, you'll usually need to register
  for **Self Assessment**.
- Check: https://www.gov.uk/guidance/tax-free-allowances-on-property-and-trading-income and
  https://www.gov.uk/check-if-you-need-tax-return
- Keep a simple spreadsheet of income and costs from day one, including your Higgsfield subscription.

## 7. Platform monetisation eligibility
- **YouTube Partner Program** (check https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851). Third-party
  guides from Oct 2026 report two tiers:
  - fan-funding: 500 subscribers plus (3,000 watch hours in 12 months **or** 3M Shorts views in 90 days)
  - ad revenue: 1,000 subscribers plus (4,000 watch hours **or** 10M Shorts views in 90 days)
  - Some guides report a stricter threshold for new applicants from Feb 2027. **Verify on YouTube's
    official page.** You also need 2-Step Verification, AdSense, and no active strikes.
- **Instagram**: monetisation tools (gifts, subscriptions, bonuses) vary by country and
  account. Check *Professional dashboard -> Monetisation status* in the app.
