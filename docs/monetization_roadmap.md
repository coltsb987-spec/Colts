# Monetisation roadmap

Honest framing: none of this is automatic or guaranteed. Most small accounts earn little for
months. These stages are milestones to aim for, not predictions. Move on when the *signals* say so,
not by the calendar.

## Stage 1 - Build an audience (months 0-3+)
**Goal:** 3-5 posts a week, learn what your audience values, build trust.
- Post every script to both Instagram and YouTube (same video, separate captions)
- Weekly review: `python3 factory.py weekly`, then double down on what has high **saves, shares and follows**
- Run a recurring series (for example, a weekly "build in public" update) so people have a reason to follow
- **Signal to move on:** people regularly ask "which tool is that?" or "how do I start?" in comments or DMs

## Stage 2 - Affiliate links (once people ask for tool recommendations)
Only recommend tools you've actually used and would recommend without a commission.

| Type | Examples to research (check UK eligibility and terms yourself) |
|---|---|
| AI and software tools | Many AI and SaaS tools run their own affiliate programmes. Look for "Affiliates" or "Partners" in a tool's website footer. Networks such as **PartnerStack** and **Impact** host many of them. |
| UK retail network | **Awin** (UK-based network with many UK merchants) |
| Books, kit (mic, lights) | **Amazon Associates UK** (affiliate-program.amazon.co.uk) |
| The tools you use | Check whether Higgsfield, your website builder or your automation tool has a creator or affiliate programme |

Rules: "Ad" at the start of the caption, say it in the video, use a link-in-bio page that
labels affiliate links, and no fake urgency. See `docs/compliance_uk.md`.

## Stage 3 - A simple digital product (once you have recurring questions)
Start with something small that answers your audience's top recurring question (see
`01_research/reports` and your own comments). Ideas that fit your content:
- **"First AI Service Starter Kit"**: offer worksheet, the one-sentence offer template, a pricing
  calculator spreadsheet, and outreach message templates (written by you, not copied)
- **"48-Hour Idea Test" Notion or Google Sheets template**
- **Prompt pack for small-business tasks**, organised by job ("reply to an enquiry", "write a quote")

How to sell: Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Stan, Payhip or similar. Compare fees and VAT handling for UK
and EU buyers before choosing. Start with a free version (lead magnet plus email signup) to test demand.
Follow UK GDPR rules for the email list.

**Claims:** describe what's inside, never promise outcomes. No fake reviews. Only use real buyer
feedback, with their permission.

## Stage 4 - An AI service or automation offer (when you can do it reliably)
A natural fit with your content. Productise it:
- One niche (for example, salons or dog groomers), one job (for example, enquiry replies plus a booking link)
- A fixed setup price and an optional monthly support fee. You set the numbers after researching
  local competitors.
- Sell the result, not "AI". Have a simple written agreement covering data protection (you may
  process client customer data).
- Document your own build: it becomes content (honest case studies with the client's permission).

## Stage 5 - Sponsorships (when you have a consistent, engaged audience)
- Typically once your views and engagement are consistent and your niche is clear. Brands look at
  audience fit and engagement, not just follower count.
- Build a one-page media kit: who your audience is, average views per Short or Reel, engagement,
  and example videos. Use real numbers from `10_analytics/content_tracker.csv`.
- Only take sponsors you'd recommend anyway. Use the Paid partnership label, "Ad" first, and disclose in the video.
- YouTube Partner Program revenue is a bonus once you're eligible (see `docs/compliance_uk.md` section 7).

## What to track for business outcomes
`content_tracker.csv` has `link_clicks` and `conversions` columns. Use a link-in-bio tool with click
counts and UTM tags on product links, so you can see which topics drive *business*, not just views.
