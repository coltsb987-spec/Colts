## Teleprompter (read this aloud)

Before you build anything, run this 48-hour test.
Most people spend weeks building something before checking that anyone actually wants it.
First, ask an AI chatbot for your customer's ten biggest frustrations. Then check them against real reviews.
Next, make a one-page offer. One problem, one promise, one price.
Then send it to ten people who fit, and ask them to join a waitlist or book a call.
If nobody's interested, you've lost two days, not two months. If a few say yes, you've got a reason to keep going.
Save this for your next idea.
