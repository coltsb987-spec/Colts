## Teleprompter (read this aloud)

Nobody wants to buy AI.
If you're starting an AI service and getting no replies, the word 'AI' might be the problem.
Business owners don't buy technology. They buy fewer missed calls, faster quotes, or an evening off.
So lead with the result. Mention the AI later, if at all.
Not 'I build AI chatbots'. Try: 'Every enquiry to your salon gets a reply, even when you're mid-appointment.'
