## Teleprompter (read this aloud)

AI won't make you money. Here's what will.
Most make-money-with-AI videos show you a tool, then skip the hard part: a real person paying you.
One. Pick a person with a problem. Not 'businesses'. Dog groomers who hate answering messages at night.
Two. Sell the result, not the tool. Nobody wakes up wanting AI. They want fewer missed bookings.
So instead of 'I do AI automation', try 'I'll make sure every enquiry gets a reply, even after hours.'
Three. Ask for money early. One paying customer teaches you more than ten new tools.
