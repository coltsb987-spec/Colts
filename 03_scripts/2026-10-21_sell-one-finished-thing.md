## Teleprompter (read this aloud)

Stop selling your time. Sell one finished thing.
'I can help with marketing' is vague. Vague offers are hard to price and hard to say yes to.
Pick one task you're already good at, like writing product descriptions.
Package it: a fixed amount, a fixed price, a fixed delivery time.
Use AI for the first draft, then your skill to edit, check and personalise. That's what people pay for.
Twenty product descriptions for your shop, delivered in three days, one fixed price. Easy to say yes to.
Comment your skill and I'll suggest a package.
